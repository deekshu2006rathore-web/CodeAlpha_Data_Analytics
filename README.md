#  Data Analytics Portfolio

---

## 🚀 Projects Overview

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on Employee Metrics
A robust EDA pipeline designed to inspect structural integrity, detect outliers, and uncover hidden trends within enterprise workforce datasets before modeling.
* **Key Achievements:** Automated check for zero-variance columns, handled missing data arrays, addressed structural categorical typos, and mapped non-linear patterns.
* **Questions Answered:**
  1. What is the baseline shape, compiler layout, and dimensional composition of the data?
  2. Does compensation scale linearly with age, or do specific structural variables skew the distribution?
  3. Are remote workers outperforming on-site staff?
* **Core Technologies:** `pandas`, `numpy`, `seaborn` matrices, `matplotlib` subplotting.

### 2. Multi-Channel Global Executive Sales Dashboard
A publication-quality 4-quadrant analytical dashboard engineered to transform row-level multi-region transactional records into clear, decision-ready business insights.
* **Key Achievements:** Built visual layers emphasizing clean typography, high data-to-ink ratios, and color palettes optimized for corporate communication.
* **Dashboard Breakdown:**
  * **Top-Left (Bar Graph):** Revenue distribution across distinct core product lines.
  * **Top-Right (Area Trend):** Macro tracking of end-of-year sales velocity over time.
  * **Bottom-Left (Donut Component):** Corporate regional market holdings and volume share.
  * **Bottom-Right (Scatter Layout):** Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) heat mapping against unit transaction values.
* **Core Technologies:** `seaborn.set_theme()`, custom matplotlib label formatters (`plt.FuncFormatter`), layout engines.

### 3. Cross-Channel Text Sentiment Intelligence System
An end-to-end Natural Language Processing (NLP) text intelligence engine configured to analyze cross-platform consumer sentiments across digital feeds.
* **Key Achievements:** Leveraged rule-based token weights to evaluate social hashtags, punctuation accents (`!`), capital scale modifiers, and emojis across **Amazon Product Reviews, Social Media Feeds, and News Headlines**.
* **Metrics Tracked:** Maps compound polarity spectrum limits spanning from `-1.0` (Extreme Negative) to `+1.0` (Extreme Positive) to inform product iteration loops.
* **Core Technologies:** NLTK Lexicon framework (`vader_lexicon`), `SentimentIntensityAnalyzer`, categorical count charting, intensity boxplot spreads.
